Combined SPOT and InReach Tracker

Live tracking data combined from both SPOT and Garmin InReach devices showing position updates throughout the Saddle Sore 1000 ride. This view displays the combined breadcrumb trail from all tracking devices.

About this tracker: This combined view aggregates tracking data from both the SPOT device (with hourly updates) and the Garmin InReach satellite communicator (with near real-time updates). The map shows the complete route with waypoints and timing information for verification purposes.

This tracker provides a comprehensive view of the entire ride, combining data from multiple GPS sources for complete verification of the Saddle Sore 1000 challenge. The map is interactive - zoom in to see detailed route information and waypoints.
