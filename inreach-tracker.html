Rob's Garmin InReach Tracker

Real-time tracking from the Garmin InReach satellite communicator with messages and location history. The InReach device provides near real-time positioning updates via satellite network.

About Garmin InReach: The Garmin InReach is a satellite communicator that enables two-way messaging and real-time position tracking. During the Saddle Sore 1000, it provided near real-time position updates, allowing tracking of the route as it happened. This serves as the most detailed tracking record for the ride.

This tracker shows the Garmin InReach data with the most frequent position updates during the ride. The detailed breadcrumb trail documents the exact route and timing, providing comprehensive verification of the Saddle Sore 1000 challenge. The map is fully interactive for detailed inspection.
